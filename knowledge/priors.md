# Priors: the looks already spent

A model with no deal designs from its prior: the average of what it has seen,
plus its own habits. Blind critics recognise both at a glance. Treat
everything below as **already used**. Each item is legitimate when the brief or
the brand asks for it explicitly (then record it in KDL `exceptions`); it is
never legitimate as the default.

## Kinetic's own habits (observed in four blind A/B rounds)
The Kinetic arms converged, brief after brief, on:
- **Warm paper neutrals + one deep teal/petrol/green accent** (`#2B6555`,
  `#17656A`, seeds near hue 170–200): 3 of 4 rounds.
- **A serif display in New York at weight 400** over SF Pro, or SF Pro Rounded
  tabular numerals as the hero.
- **Single-column narrative**: context line → large title/numeral → one inset
  grouped list → docked action. Correct, calm, and judged *forgettable*
  (distinctiveness 3 vs 4 for the bare model in every round).
- **Identity as one quiet detail** (a numeral, a tint) rather than a world.
Your first palette, first face and first layout are these. Deal instead.

## Generated-app clusters (all models)
- **Cream and clay**: a cream/parchment ground (`#F4F1EA`, `#FAF7F0`), a
  high-contrast serif display, a terracotta or clay accent (`#D97757`).
- **Night neon**: near-black ground with one acid-green, lime or vermilion accent.
- **Violet AI**: indigo/violet gradients (`#7C3AED`, `#6366F1`), glass cards.
- **Soft wellness**: sage + peach + rounded sans + blob illustrations.
- **The SaaS card kit on a phone**: every block a rounded white card with the
  same grey shadow, one radius everywhere.
- **Template chrome**: tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels over every section,
  meta strings joined by middle dots everywhere, small monospace labels as
  "technical" decoration, `→` appended to every button.

## Accent priors
- **Link blue as the accent** (#1E4FD8–#2F6BFF, saturated royal/cobalt) on a
  warm or earthy product reads as "default" to blind critics (round 8: "the
  one element that would survive a swap"). Take the accent from the domain's
  materials (soil, ink, glaze, signal paint), unless the domain is ink or water.
- **A dark hero slab kept in light mode** (charcoal header on a light page)
  makes "light mode" a dark screen with a light footer: design the light hero.

## Home-screen priors (mobile)
- Greeting header ("Good morning, Sam") → summary card → 2×2 tile grid → list.
- Big number + small label + sparkline + "+12% this week" chip.
- A horizontal row of filter chips under the title on every screen.
- A five-tab bar with outline icons where two areas exist.
- A gradient hero card with a white CTA.

## Spent faces (as display or brand voice)
Inter, Inter Display, Fraunces, Instrument Serif/Sans, Playfair Display,
Space Grotesk, DM Sans/Serif, IBM Plex, Manrope, Poppins, Montserrat,
Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, Sora, Geist. The scanner flags them (D33). The
platform text faces (SF Pro, Roboto) are *not* spent: native text is a virtue;
the voice belongs in the display role (knowledge/library/type.json).

## When a prior is right
A prior is spent as a *default*, not as a choice. When the brief's own tone
words ask for it — warm, local, bookish, heritage, cinephile, handmade — a
paper ground and a serif voice can be exactly right (blind critics rewarded
them in round 6). Then make them specific: this shop's bag paper, this
cinema's programme type, this studio's clay — not the generic cream and
terracotta. The test is the swap test, not the palette.

## How to use this file
1. Before converging, compare each dealt option with the lists above: an option
   that lands back on a prior after your mutations has been averaged — undo the
   mutation, not the deal.
2. In self-critique, cover the logo and ask which cluster the screen belongs
   to. If you can name one, distinctiveness is ≤ 3.
