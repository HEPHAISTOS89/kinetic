# Kinetic v17: candid feedback from the Mooring run (studio, create, D2)

1. **`kinetic clarity` reads drawn vocabulary as text.** It is a pixel/edge heuristic. The banded sill post, the windsock stripes and the slide sign were counted as "lines/words/text sizes". Light and dark twins with identical copy differed by 5–16 points: a white post on a light ground breaks into short "lines", while on a dark ground it is one "graphic". Adding a dark timber pile beside the post (a legitimate detail) raised the light score, which is close to gaming the metric. The protocol makes clarity < 70 block `ship` and caps layout_craft. So the engine penalises exactly what kinetic-direct §4b asks for: drawn marks at ≥ 4 sites, the hero included. It needs a mask for declared vocabulary regions, or OCR.
2. **The redraw re-dealt the same failure.** I redrew option 1's palette because its navy #14213D broke `thesis.never` (yachting navy). The redraw returned ultramarine #1E2A78. The second redraw kept a navy accent #1A2B6D and only added a yellow field. The deal is "weighted by tone", but a redraw doesn't exclude hues close to the one rejected.
3. **The loop cap and staleness conflict.** The studio protocol caps at 3 loops. The user (rightly) asks for the panel's consensus fixes to be done. `kinetic score` then reports ITERATE with "judgment predates the current renders", and that can only be cleared by a 4th panel. Mean across loops: 3.7, then 3.8, then 3.8. layout_craft and native_fidelity sat at 3 throughout, partly because of item 1 and partly because the render harness only renders Android/Roboto. No rule says how to close out honestly.
4. **Critics, editor and router disagree on fields and copy.**
   - Editors cut "She needs 1.5 m on the post" twice as redundant. The panel's consensus then asked to state the margin ("Draws 1.2 m, needs 1.5 m").
   - Editors cut "fee paid"; the panel asked for "paid" beside the sticker.
   - Critics also handled `motion` inconsistently. One left it unscored per PROTOCOL §6 ("unjudged"). Others scored it from stills while also listing it as unjudged. `kinetic panel` then merged 2 votes for motion in loop 1.
   - Editor and critics need one shared rule on redundancy versus explanation.
5. **No sector for this product.** sectors.md has no marina, harbour or tide entry. The nearest are "Transit" (windows/departures) and "Community venue" (staff by name). Both were useful, but the router says "read your sector" and there wasn't one. A "safety-critical outdoor/maritime" lens (sill/tide windows, weather as a go/no-go) would have prompted the gale-plus-verdict interaction earlier. A critic caught it only in loop 3.
6. **The Flutter golden gotcha hit as documented, but late.** Material button `textStyle`s without `fontFamily` rendered as Ahem boxes in the first render. stacks/flutter.md mentions this in "Golden/gallery gotchas". The exported `kineticTheme` sets `TextTheme(bodyLarge: KType.body, labelLarge: …)` with no family, so anyone wiring the exported tokens into button themes falls into it. The exporter could emit the platform family or document it.
7. **`TextScaler.noScaling` vs painted figures.** Figures painted on a mark (metres on the post, the berth stencil) need a bounded scale. The F10 rule pushes you to `clamp`, which I used, but no guidance says that marks may clamp. I had to add `markScaler()` myself.
8. **Small engine and doc frictions:**
   - `kinetic sheet` writes `contact-sheet.png` into the CWD (the project root) unless given `--out`, and the studio skill doesn't say where it should live.
   - `kinetic contrast` takes two separate args (fine), but zsh users calling it in a loop trip over word-splitting. A `fg:bg` form would help.
   - K21 counts the header/freshness line in `reading_order` and then flags must-haves at position #5. Marking the header `pinned: true` was the workaround; the docs don't say so.
   - The `exceptions`/data-colour route for painted-object constants (post paint that must not change with theme) wasn't obvious. D13 flagged it, and `system.color.data` with `{light, dark, on}` was the right home. That's worth a sentence in color.md.
9. **The director agent's tools are read-only** (`tools: [Read, Glob, Grep]` in kinetic-director.md), yet the studio protocol says to "save their outputs to .kinetic/studio/director-N.md". Launched as general-purpose agents they could write, but the agent definitions contradict the protocol.
10. **Rival and directors were useful; the deal was half useful.** Dealt challenger B (score-sheet grid) was correctly judged non-viable for a phone home (6×4 grid, usability 3). Dealt challenger C (verdict sentence) mostly converged with the authored direction. The authored direction won, and only two *rules* were borrowed from the deal. The 2-option deal gave little divergence on a brief whose own inventory was strong.
11. **No recording path.** Motion and the signature crossing can't be judged from goldens. A `kinetic` helper to render a frame strip from a widget test (pump at t = 0, 150, 300 ms…) would let critics judge motion instead of marking it unjudged every loop.
