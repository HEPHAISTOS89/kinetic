**Kinetic v1.1 lost round 2.** Its self-judged 4.00 was again more lenient than the independent critics (3.56).

## Causes named by both critics
1. Color: black-and-grey chrome with black buttons, judged generic. Root cause: our own transit playbook said "chrome neutral".
2. A signature that needs a legend ("Large times: reachable on foot"), the same failure as round 1's run chip.
3. A large "Departures" title spending the top of a glance screen. Root cause: our iOS guidance recommended large titles without nuance.
4. Stale/offline numbers at full weight (both arms).
5. No user-scoped disruption summary (the baseline had "Affects your trip to Work").

## Kinetic still led on
Breadth of states (server vs connection error, AX layout, long names, progressive loading), condensed-numeral voice,
inline closures with alternatives, and measured system rigor (0 scan findings vs the baseline's 3 major).

## Changes (v1.3)
D31 legend-dependent encoding + a legibility test for signatures; "monochrome is a thesis, not a default";
a quiet chrome that still has color in the transit playbook; glance screens spend their top on answers;
iOS large-title nuance; stale data must look stale; user-scoped disruptions; rows ordered by catchability.
